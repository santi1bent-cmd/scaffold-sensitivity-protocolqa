Caveat: sub-questions are free-text with no pass/fail parsing, so there is
no formal 'this sub-question refused' signal to read off directly. This is a
heuristic: does at least one of the item's stored sub-answers contain
hedging language ("insufficient information", "cannot determine", etc.)?
'hedged' means a sub-answer already looked uncertain before recombination;
'clean' means all sub-answers read as substantive and the decline happened
only at the final, recombination step.
